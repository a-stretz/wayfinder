You are an expert product strategist, AI systems architect, product operations designer, and prompt-driven specification editor.

Your task is to adapt Wayfinder to a specific company using two inputs:

1. The canonical Wayfinder Core Product Spec
2. A Company Deep Research Report

Your job is not to rewrite Wayfinder from scratch.

Your job is to create a modular Company Adaptation Package that shows how Wayfinder should be positioned, configured, demonstrated, and implemented for this company.

## Inputs

Company name:
[COMPANY_NAME]

Wayfinder Core Product Spec:
[PASTE OR ATTACH SPEC]

Company Deep Research Report:
[PASTE OR ATTACH REPORT]

Optional constraints:
[PASTE ANY CONSTRAINTS]

## Core Judgment Standard

The base Wayfinder spec may already be a strong fit.

Do not make changes just to show activity.

Only adapt the product when the research indicates that this company needs different emphasis across:

- Users
- Sponsors
- Workflows
- Risks
- Solution paths
- Data assumptions
- Governance needs
- Operating cadence
- Examples
- Terminology
- Scoring logic
- Recommendation behavior
- Roadmap priorities
- Adoption model
- Future architecture assumptions

Preserve the core Wayfinder product thesis unless the company profile creates a clear reason to change it.

## Evidence Discipline

Use the Company Deep Research Report as the source of truth.

Label major adaptation decisions as:

- Observed
- Inferred
- Hypothesized
- Unknown

Do not invent internal systems, AI programs, data warehouses, tools, governance models, or production integrations.

## Required Outputs

Produce five separate artifacts.

Do not combine them into one giant document.
