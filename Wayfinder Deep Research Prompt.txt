# Company Deep Research Prompt for Wayfinder Adaptation

You are an expert company research analyst, product strategist, and AI transformation researcher.

Your task is to produce a deep research report on the following company so that a product strategy system called Wayfinder can be adapted to that company.

Wayfinder is an internal initiative intelligence system. It helps organizations capture scattered operational signals, workflow pain points, internal requests, dashboard asks, automation ideas, AI opportunities, lightweight tools, and strategic initiatives, then convert them into structured initiatives, recommendations, solution paths, candidate requirements, governance visibility, and pattern learning.

## Research Objective

Research the company deeply enough to understand how Wayfinder should be positioned, adapted, demonstrated, and configured for this company.

## Evidence Discipline

Label major claims as:

- Observed: directly supported by public evidence or provided material
- Inferred: reasonable interpretation based on evidence
- Hypothesized: plausible opportunity, but not confirmed
- Unknown: insufficient evidence

Do not invent:

- Internal systems
- AI programs
- Departments
- Data warehouses
- Governance models
- Tech stack details
- Executive priorities
- Workflow ownership
- Existing initiatives
- Production integrations

When evidence is insufficient, say Unknown.

## Required Research Sections

## 1. Executive Summary

Summarize:

- What the company does
- How it creates value
- Why its operating model may or may not fit Wayfinder
- The highest-value areas for internal initiative intelligence
- The most important uncertainties

## 2. Company and Operating Model

Answer:

- What does the company actually do?
- What are the major business lines?
- Who are its customers, users, tenants, clients, partners, or stakeholders?
- How does the company make money?
- What makes the operating model complex?
- What workflows likely require cross-functional coordination?
- Where might internal software, automation, dashboards, AI, or lightweight tools create value?

## 5. AI, Data, and Product Maturity Assessment

Assess likely maturity.

Use this scale:

- Level 0: No visible digital or AI readiness
- Level 1: Basic systems and manual reporting
- Level 2: Some structured systems and reporting
- Level 3: Active analytics, automation, or internal tooling
- Level 4: Formal product, data, or AI operating model
- Level 5: Scaled AI-native operating model

Include:

- Likely current level
- Evidence
- Confidence
- What would need to be true to move up one level
- Whether the company appears centralized, decentralized, or hybrid
